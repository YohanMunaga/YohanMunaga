# Findings and interpretation

## Sample results
In this fabricated dataset, churn appears more common among month-to-month customers than among customers on longer contracts. The sample is deliberately small and designed only to demonstrate analysis.

## Recommended follow-up
- Validate patterns against a representative, governed dataset.
- Segment by acquisition channel, service usage, support contacts, and price changes.
- Check cohort and time effects, missingness, and selection bias.
- Design a controlled retention test before attributing outcomes to an intervention.

## Limitations
This is descriptive analysis of invented records. It does not represent any employer, customer base, or measured business result. Association is not causation.
